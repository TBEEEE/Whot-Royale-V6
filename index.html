How to play

Play a card that matches the top card's shape or number. Can't play? Draw one card and your turn ends. Empty your hand to win the round and score the value of everyone else's cards.

  • 1Hold On. Play again.
  • 2Pick Two. Next player draws 2 and loses their turn.
  • 5Pick Three. Next player draws 3 and loses their turn.
  • 8Suspension. The next player is skipped.
  • 14General Market. Everyone else draws 1.
  • 20WHOT. Plays on anything. You choose the next shape.
