# WHOT Royale: competition description

**WHOT Royale** is a free, real-time online version of WHOT, the Nigerian card game played at family gatherings and in every neighbourhood. Open the link, type a nickname, and you're at the table: no signup, no download, nothing to install. Share a six-character room code (or tap Invite) and two to six players can play together from their own phones, tablets or computers. Prefer strangers? Quick match seats you with other players and starts the game for you.

**Nigerian rules.** Hold On (1), Pick Two (2), Pick Three (5), Suspension (8), General Market (14) and the WHOT wild (20, where you choose the next shape) all work the way people play them. Scores carry across rounds, and anyone at the table can call the rematch.

**Built for real connections.** The game server decides every move and each player only ever receives their own cards, so nobody can peek or cheat. Refresh the page or lose signal and you return to your seat with your hand intact. If a player drops, the table isn't stuck: their turn is played for them after a few seconds. Table chat and quick replies like "Oya, play!" keep the banter going, and sound effects mark every play, penalty and win.

**Look and feel.** An emerald felt table with gold trim and cream cards, with a distinct colour and shape on every card so it reads at a glance on a small screen.

**Tech.** Node.js, Express and Socket.IO; pure-JavaScript rules engine; 51 automated tests including a six-player game over live WebSocket connections and a three-browser end-to-end check.
